First release of `cosmoz-tree-view`: a tree of nested, expandable rows after the Untitled UI tree view. Click to select, chevron to expand, double-click or Enter to activate; full WAI-ARIA tree keyboard support; only expanded branches are walked and rows are virtualized, so trees with hundreds of thousands of nodes stay fast. Controlled or uncontrolled `selected` / `expanded`, custom data accessors, optional icons, `sm` / `md` sizes, CSS parts for styling.
